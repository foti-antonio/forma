## Tester instructions

Read [BETA](docs/BETA.md) and [SECURITY-MODEL](docs/SECURITY-MODEL.md), opt in
through the future coordinator, receive the exact assigned candidate and reviewed
checksum list, verify before installation, and use a clean test account with
sample workspaces and disposable repositories. Complete one acceptance sheet per
platform/package/upgrade pair. Agent testing is individually optional and needs
separate informed consent; required coverage is assigned to consenting testers.

Unsigned Windows/ad-hoc macOS packages can be blocked by ordinary trust policy.
Stop and report Blocked; never bypass SmartScreen/Gatekeeper, strip quarantine or
weaken security. Backups/recovery copies are unencrypted and retained until
removed. Agents/tests execute with OS-user host access; worktrees provide no OS
sandbox. Submit only sanitized technical results through the agreed private
channel; never credentials, private repositories, raw backups or sensitive logs.
